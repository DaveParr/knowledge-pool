# Context

Terms used in this codebase.

- **Card catalogue** (`cards.sv.jac`): the one module that looks cards up, resolves card names and keeps `Card` nodes as a cache of the SQLite catalogue; `RefreshCards` updates that cache after a re-ingest. Only it calls `catalog.store`.
- **Deck holdings**: the cards a deck holds, as `Holds` edges (a count in one zone, plus entry metadata). They change only through `change_holding` (one card in one zone) or `replace_holdings` (the whole list) in `decks.sv.jac`, which check the zone, count and reason and log each count change as a `ChangeNote`.
- **Commander**: the format where a deck is the commander zone plus the main deck, exactly 100 cards, singleton, led by one eligible commander. Its rules are `check_commander` in `legality.sv.jac`; the commander zone helpers and eligibility live in `commander.sv.jac`.
- **Partner pair**: two commanders that may lead one Commander deck together (Partner, Partner with, Partner—quality, Choose a Background with a Background, Doctor's companion with a Time Lord Doctor). `pair_problem` in `commander.sv.jac` returns why a pair is illegal, or `""`.
- **Zone**: where in a deck a card is held: one of `ZONES` in `decks.sv.jac` (commander, companion, main, sideboard, maybeboard). Constructed formats count main and sideboard, and Commander counts the commander zone and main deck (checking the companion too).
- **Change note**: one logged change to a deck's holdings (card, zone, count before and after, and the reason). Kept on the `Deck` as its history.
- **Deck view** (`DeckView`, `deck_view` in `decks.sv.jac`): the one read of a deck that every deck walker reports: its card lines, totals, legality, recent history and main-deck mana analysis (mana profile and curve). A client gets the whole picture from the edit's own response; MCP `deck_analyze` is just the `mana` and `curve` part of it, plus the name, format and main total its chart view needs.
- **Card line** (`CardLine`, `card_lines.sv.jac`): one card in one zone of a deck, with its count, entry metadata and catalogue facts. `card_lines(deck)` in `decks.sv.jac` is the one place lines are built from the graph; legality (`check_lines`), the mana profile and the curve are pure functions over a deck's lines, and `DeckView.cards` is that list. It holds the one definition of a **land** (`is_land`: the front face is a land, so a spell // land double-faced card is a spell) and of the **main deck** (`main_deck`: the main zone).
- **Walker error** (`WalkerError`, `walker_errors.sv.jac`): why a walker failed, as its one and only report: a stable `code` (e.g. `no-such-deck`, `unknown-format`, `unknown-list-format`, `no-import`, `refused-change`) and a `message` to show as is. `DeckAction` reports `no-such-deck` for every deck walker; MCP tools pass it on as `{"error", "code"}` (`outcome` in `mcp_server.jac`) and the web reads it with `failureMessage` (`walker_failure.cl.jac`).
