Pool icons

Knowledge Pool’s own icons: one ink stroke on a 24px grid, round joins, drawn here and owned by the app. They stand in for words that repeat on every row, like “missing”, “sleeveable” and “58 to go”. Words stay where something needs explaining: headers, the card view, and screen-reader labels.
